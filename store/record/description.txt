How it works: Neat Record captures what you choose with Chrome's own screen-capture tools and encodes the video on your computer. Nothing is uploaded unless you press Share (Pro), which uploads that one video to your own Google Drive and gives you a link anyone can watch. Neat Record can only see files it created in your Drive, never anything else.
